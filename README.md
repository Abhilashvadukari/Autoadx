# AutoAdX 🚖📺

## India's Mobile Digital Advertising Network

AutoAdX is an innovative advertising platform that transforms auto-rickshaws into moving digital billboards using rear-mounted LED display screens.

The platform enables businesses to launch hyperlocal advertising campaigns by selecting specific cities and target areas through a centralized dashboard. Advertisements are displayed on digital screens installed on auto-rickshaws while they operate normally across the city.

Auto drivers earn additional passive income through campaign participation, while advertisers gain affordable, highly visible, and location-based advertising.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional billboards are expensive and fixed to one location.

Digital advertising platforms are becoming increasingly crowded, expensive, and often ignored by users.

Small and medium businesses struggle to reach nearby customers effectively.

---

## Solution

AutoAdX combines:

* Digital Out-of-Home Advertising (DOOH)
* GPS-Based Targeting
* Mobile Advertising Networks
* Driver Monetization
* Real-Time Campaign Management

Businesses can:

1. Upload advertisements
2. Select target locations
3. Launch campaigns instantly
4. Track campaign performance
